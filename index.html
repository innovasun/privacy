Privacy Policy – Vision Innova.Sun

Effective date: 5 November 2026

In short
  • Everything you enter and every result stays on your phone, encrypted.
  • We do not collect, receive, upload, share or sell any of your data.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • You can delete all of it at any time.

1. Who we are

Vision Innova.Sun (“the app”) is made by Innova.Sun (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information. Contact: admin@innovasun.tech.

2. Information the app stores on your phone

To run the vision checks and show your results, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Profile: your name, date of birth, whether you wear glasses for distance or reading, and (optional) your sex.
  • Screen measurement: the size of your phone’s screen as measured with a card, your phone model and Android version — so letters are drawn at the correct size.
  • Vision checks: your answers during each check, your results, and the date and time.
  • Your consent: the date you agreed to this policy and which version you agreed to.

We use this information only to run the checks, explain your results and show how your vision changes over time.

3. What we receive

Nothing. The app has no servers and no user accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. None of the information above is sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

4. How your information is protected

  • It is stored in an encrypted database on your phone. The encryption key is kept in Android’s secure Keystore.
  • The app does not use Android cloud backup, so your information is not copied to any online backup.

5. Permissions

The app does not currently ask for any special permissions (such as camera, location, contacts or microphone). If a future version needs one, we will explain why in the app and update this policy first.

6. Sharing

We do not share your information. If you choose to export or share a report or your data from the app, it goes only where you send it.

7. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Delete my data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it permanently. Because we never hold a copy, deleted information cannot be recovered.

8. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to have your grievances addressed. In this app you can:

  • See and correct your profile in Settings → Your profile.
  • Export your data in Settings → Export my data.
  • Erase your data, which also withdraws your consent, in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.

For any question or complaint about your data, email admin@innovasun.tech. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Adults only

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it.

10. Not medical advice

Vision Innova.Sun is a vision check for general wellness. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose any eye condition, and it does not replace an examination by an eye doctor.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, the app will ask you to agree again before you continue.
